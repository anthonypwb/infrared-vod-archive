Infrared VOD Archive

A personal automation tool that preserves available Infrared livestream recordings on the operator’s YouTube archive channel.

Watch the archive on YouTube →

What the app does

The app discovers source recordings, downloads available video, uploads it to the designated archive channel, and checks that YouTube has processed the upload. It keeps an archive index to avoid duplicate uploads.

Why it requests Google access

Only the archive operator signs in. The app uses YouTube upload permission to publish videos and read-only YouTube permission to confirm the destination channel, find existing uploads, and check upload status. Viewers do not need to connect their Google accounts to this website.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for details about Google data, storage, and revoking access, and the terms of service for use of the app. For questions or removal requests, contact talonsight@gmail.com.

This is an independently operated archive tool and is not a Google or YouTube product.
