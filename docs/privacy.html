Privacy policy

This policy describes how Infrared VOD Archive handles information. The app is a personal archive uploader operated by the owner of the @archiveinfrared YouTube channel. Contact: talonsight@gmail.com.

Google and YouTube data

The operator authorizes the app through Google OAuth. The app requests YouTube upload and read-only access. It uses authorization tokens to upload recordings and reads the connected channel’s identifier, upload eligibility, uploads playlist, video metadata, visibility, duration, and processing status to verify the destination and avoid duplicate uploads. It does not request Gmail, Google Contacts, or Google Drive access.

Use and storage

Google data is used to operate and verify the archive. OAuth client credentials and the refresh token are stored as GitHub Actions secrets and supplied to the archive runner when needed. Access tokens and resumable-upload session URLs are used during execution and are not intentionally written to the archive index. Video files are processed on the runner for transfer to YouTube.

The public GitHub repository stores archive records, including source information, YouTube video identifiers, processing outcomes, and duration checks. Records and workflow logs can remain in repository history and GitHub retention systems. Uploaded videos and their published metadata are available through YouTube according to their visibility settings.

Sharing and service providers

Google/YouTube processes authorization requests and receives uploaded videos and metadata. GitHub hosts the website, code, automation, secrets, and archive records. These services may process technical information such as IP addresses under their own policies. The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Google user data is used and transferred only as necessary to provide the archive functionality or comply with applicable law.

Infrared VOD Archive’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

The refresh token is retained until replaced or removed so scheduled archiving can continue. Archive records are retained to track completed uploads and prevent duplicates. The operator can revoke access in Google Account connections and remove the GitHub secrets to stop future authorized access. Revoking access does not delete existing YouTube uploads or public archive records.

For deletion or removal requests, email talonsight@gmail.com. Public records may also exist in Git history, forks, caches, and provider backups; removing a current record does not automatically remove those copies. Videos can be managed separately in YouTube Studio.

Website visitors

This website has no account registration, advertising, or operator-added analytics. GitHub Pages serves the website. Following links to YouTube, Google, or GitHub subjects your interaction to those services’ policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
